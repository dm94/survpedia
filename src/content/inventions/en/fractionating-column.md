# Brief description

A fractionating column is an essential separation device used in fractional distillation to separate chemical mixtures into their individual component fractions based on differences in boiling points. It achieves high-purity separations through repeated vaporizations and condensations along its height.

## Use / Function

- **Petroleum Refining:** Separates crude oil into gasoline, kerosene, diesel, lubricating oils, and heavy fuel oils.
- **Alcohol Purification:** Concentrates fermented bio-ethanol solutions up to high-purity industrial or fuel-grade alcohol levels.
- **Air Separation:** Distils liquid air at cryogenic temperatures to produce pure nitrogen, oxygen, and argon gases.
- **Scale:** From glass laboratory columns to massive industrial refinery distillation towers.

## Operating principle

1. **Boiling Phase:** A liquid mixture is boiled at the base in a reboiler or distillation flask, generating rising multi-component vapors.
2. **Rising Vapor & Downflowing Liquid:** Vapors ascend through the fractionating column while condensed liquid (reflux) flows downward over internal packing or trays.
3. **Repeated Equilibrium Steps:** At each tray or packing surface, hotter rising vapor transfers heat to cooler downflowing liquid. Lower boiling point components vaporize and continue upward, while higher boiling point components condense and flow downward.
4. **Temperature Gradient:** A continuous temperature gradient forms, ranging from hot at the column base to cooler at the top.
5. **Fraction Collection:** Low-boiling point fractions exit the column head as pure vapor to be condensed, while higher-boiling fractions exit at lower side taps or return to the reboiler.

## How to create it

1. **Construct Column Cylinder:** Fabricate a vertical tube or cylinder from heat-resistant glass, copper pipe, or stainless steel casing.
2. **Insert Packing Material or Trays:** Fill the column interior with high surface area packing (such as glass beads, Raschig rings, or copper mesh) or install perforated horizontal trays.
3. **Attach Reboiler Kettle:** Mount the lower end of the column to a heated boiling kettle equipped with a liquid level indicator.
4. **Install Column Head & Condenser:** Connect the top outlet of the column to a total condenser cooled by circulating water and a reflux splitter valve.
5. **Incorporate Temperature Sensors:** Place thermometers or pyrometer sensors at the base, middle sections, and head of the column to monitor fractional temperatures.
6. **Insulate and Operate:** Apply thermal insulation to the column walls, heat the mixture, adjust the reflux ratio, and collect separated fractions.

## Materials needed

- **Column Body:** Borosilicate glass, copper tube, or stainless steel pipe. Sourced from **[Glass](/en/materials/glass)**, **[Copper](/en/materials/copper)**, and **[Steel](/en/materials/steel)**.
- **Packing & Trays:** Glass beads, ceramic rings, or copper wire mesh.
- **Condenser & Heat Source:** Chilled water condenser and controllable boiler heater. Sourced from **[Water](/en/materials/water)**.

## Variants and improvements

- **Vigreux Column:** Laboratory glass column with internal indented glass fingers pointing downward to provide internal condensation surface area without loose packing.
- **Packed Tower:** Industrial column filled with structured ceramic or metal packing that maximizes liquid-vapor contact area.
- **Bubble-Cap Tray Column:** Industrial distillation column using horizontal trays with perforated bubble caps to force vapor to bubble through pools of liquid on each plate.

## Limits and risks

- **Azeotropic Limits:** Binary mixtures that form azeotropes (such as ethanol-water at 95.6% purity) cannot be separated beyond their azeotropic concentration by simple fractional distillation.
- **Flammable Vapor Leaks:** Volatile hydrocarbons and alcohol vapors pose severe fire and explosion risks if joints leak near heat sources.
- **Thermal Flooding:** Excessive heating rates can cause liquid to pool in the column ("flooding"), ruining fractional separation efficiency.
