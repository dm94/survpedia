# Brief description

A polarimeter is an optical instrument that measures the angle of optical rotation caused by passing polarized light through optically active substances (such as sugar solutions, amino acids, or essential oils). It is an essential analytical tool for chemical purity and concentration testing.

## Use / Function

- **Sugar Industry (Saccharimetry):** Determining the precise sugar concentration (sucrose/glucose) in sugarcane juice and syrups.
- **Chemical Analysis:** Identifying chiral molecules, stereoisomers, and monitoring chemical reaction rates (e.g., sugar inversion).
- **Pharmaceutical & Food Control:** Verifying the purity and quality of oils, essential essences, and active drug compounds.

## Operating principle

1. **Light Polarizing:** Unpolarized light passes through a polarizing filter (such as a Nicol prism or sheet polarizer), restricting the light waves to vibrate in a single plane.
2. **Optical Rotation:** The plane-polarized light enters a transparent tube filled with an optically active solution. Chiral molecules in the liquid rotate the plane of polarization by a specific angle ($\alpha$).
3. **Analyzing Light:** The rotated beam hits a second polarizing filter (the analyzer) mounted on a rotatable circular scale.
4. **Extinction Measurement:** By rotating the analyzer until maximum darkness (extinction) or equal brightness is restored, the angle of rotation is read directly off the scale. The rotation angle is directly proportional to the concentration ($C$) and tube length ($L$).

## How to create it

1. **Monochromatic Light Source:** Position a sodium vapor lamp or yellow-filtered light source at the entrance of a light-tight tube.
2. **Polarizer & Analyzer Prisms:** Mount two calcite prisms (Nicol prisms) or high-quality polarizing sheets at opposite ends of an optical bench.
3. **Sample Tube:** Construct a glass tube with flat, strain-free glass end caps to hold liquid samples without introducing artificial stress birefringence.
4. **Protractor Scale:** Attach a 360-degree circular protractor scale with a vernier reader to the rotatable analyzer mount.
5. **Eyepiece Assembly:** Add a magnifying lens or split-field half-shadow plate (Laurent plate) to enhance human eye sensitivity when determining equal brightness.

## Materials needed

- **Essential:** Clear **[Glass](/en/materials/glass)** tube with polished end caps, calcite crystal or synthetic polarizing filters, **[Brass](/en/materials/brass)** mounts and protractor scale, **[Wood](/en/materials/wood)** or metal base.
- **Tools:** Lathe, glass grinding/polishing tools, fine graduated protractor.
- **Substitutes:** Modern synthetic polarizing film sheets (from LCD screens) can substitute for natural calcite Nicol prisms.

## Variants and improvements

- **Half-Shadow Polarimeter (Laurent):** Uses a quartz half-wave plate to split the field into two halves, making it vastly easier to detect the balance point with the human eye.
- **Automatic Photoelectric Polarimeter:** Uses electronic photodetectors and Faraday rotators to automatically measure rotation angles to thousandths of a degree.

## Limits and risks

- **Strain Birefringence:** Stress in glass end caps causes artificial light rotation, creating measurement errors.
- **Turbid Samples:** Cloudy or dark liquids absorb or scatter light, making fringe observation difficult unless filtered or diluted first.
- **Temperature Dependence:** Optical rotation varies with temperature and wavelength; measurements must be standardized (typically at 20°C and the sodium D-line 589 nm).
