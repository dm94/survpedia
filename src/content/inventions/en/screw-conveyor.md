# Brief description

A continuous mechanical handling mechanism that utilizes a rotating helical screw blade ("flighting") within a trough or tube to convey granular, powdered, or semi-solid bulk materials horizontally or at an incline.

## Use / Function

- **Primary Use:** Transporting grain, flour, cement, wood chips, coal ash, and animal feed across processing facilities.
- **Secondary Uses:** Mixing, heating, or cooling bulk ingredients while in transit; feeding crushed rock or ore into industrial hoppers and kilns.
- **Scale:** Agricultural farms, flour mills, chemical plants, and construction sites.

## Operating principle

1. **Material Feeding:** Bulk material enters through an open inlet hopper at one end of the trough.
2. **Rotational Shear:** A central shaft fitted with continuous helical flighting turns continuously driven by a gearmotor.
3. **Axial Displacement:** The helical flighting acts as an inclined plane in continuous rotation, pushing material along the inner wall of the stationary trough without letting it rotate freely with the screw.
4. **Discharge:** The conveyed material reaches an open discharge spout at the opposite end and falls out by gravity.

## How to create it

1. **Fabricate the Screw Shaft:** Secure a solid steel shaft or thick-walled pipe to serve as the central core.
2. **Form Helical Flights:** Cut annular steel rings, split each ring radially, and stretch them into helical pitch segments before welding them continuously along the shaft.
3. **Construct Trough or Tube:** Form a U-shaped trough or cylindrical housing from sheet steel or hollowed wood matching the radius of the screw flighting.
4. **Install End Bearings and Seals:** Mount thrust bearings and dust seals at both end plates to support the shaft loads and retain fine powders.
5. **Drive System:** Connect a pulley, sprocket, or gear speed reducer to the drive end shaft.

## Materials needed

- **Helical Flighting & Shaft:** Heavy structural **[Steel](/en/materials/steel)** or forged **[Iron](/en/materials/iron)**.
- **Trough Housing:** Formed sheet **[Steel](/en/materials/steel)** or heavy carved **[Wood](/en/materials/wood)**.
- **Bearings & Mounts:** Cast **[Iron](/en/materials/iron)** bearing housings and bronze bushings.

## Variants and improvements

- **Tubular Screw Conveyor:** Fully enclosed pipe structure enabling steep incline transport (up to 90 degrees) and dust-free containment.
- **Shaftless Screw Conveyor:** Uses a heavy-duty spiral without a central pipe shaft, preventing sticky, stringy materials (such as sewage sludge) from entangling.
- **Archimedes Water Screw:** Ancient open-tube variant used for lifting water from low-lying irrigation ditches.

## Limits and risks

- **Material Degradation:** Fragile or friable grains can be crushed or bruised between the screw flight edge and the trough wall.
- **High Power Requirement:** Friction between bulk materials and casing creates resistance, limiting single-unit transport distance.
- **Pinch and Entanglement Hazard:** Exposed rotating screws pose severe limb amputation hazards; solid safety guards are mandatory.
