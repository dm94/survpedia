# Brief description

A mechanical device with rotating cylinders covered in fine wire teeth that disentangles, cleans, and intermixes raw fibers (such as wool or cotton) to produce a continuous web or sliver suitable for spinning into yarn.

## Use / Function

- **Textile Preparation:** Essential precursor to mechanical spinning; converts tangled raw fleece or harvested cotton into ordered fibers.
- **Fiber Blending:** Mixes fibers of different colors or types evenly before spinning.
- **Mass Production:** Increases textile fiber processing capacity hundreds of times compared to hand carding.

## Operating principle

1.  **Feeding:** Raw, washed, and oiled raw fibers are fed on a belt into feed rollers.
2.  **Teasing & Straightening:** A large main cylinder (drum) covered in thousands of bent wire pins rotates rapidly, pulling fibers across smaller counter-rotating cylinders (workers and strippers).
3.  **Alignment:** The opposing wire teeth tease apart knots and align the individual fibers parallel to each other.
4.  **Doffing:** A specialized comb or doffing roller strips the aligned web of fibers off the drum, condensing it into a rope-like sliver.

## How to create it

1.  **Wooden Frame & Drums:** Build a sturdy frame and turn wooden cylinders mounted on metal axles.
2.  **Card Clothing:** Produce leather or heavy fabric strips embedded with rows of angled fine steel wires (wire teeth).
3.  **Drum Assembly:** Wrap and secure the card clothing around the main cylinder and smaller satellite rollers.
4.  **Gear Drive:** Connect the rollers with a system of gears or pulley belts so they rotate at precise relative speeds.
5.  **Doffing Comb:** Mount a reciprocating comb or wire roller at the output to collect the carded web.

## Materials needed

- **Frame & Cylinders:** **[Wood](/en/materials/wood)** or **[Iron](/en/materials/iron)**.
- **Card Clothing:** **[Leather](/en/materials/leather)** or heavy **[Fabric](/en/materials/fabric)**.
- **Teeth:** Hardened **[Steel](/en/materials/steel)** wire.
- **Transmission:** Belts, pulleys, or iron **[Gears](/en/inventions/gears)**.

## Variants and improvements

- **Hand Carding Paddles:** Ancient flat wooden boards with wire teeth used manually.
- **Roller Carding Machine:** Uses multiple worker and stripper rollers surrounding a central main drum.
- **Flat Carding Machine:** Uses stationary or revolving flat wire bars over the main drum; ideal for short cotton fibers.

## Limits and risks

- **Wire Maintenance:** Teeth dull or bend over time, requiring frequent stripping and sharpening (grinding).
- **Fire Hazard:** Dry plant/wool fibers and high-speed friction present a severe ignition risk.
- **Entanglement:** Rapidly moving rollers can trap fingers and hands if unguarded.
