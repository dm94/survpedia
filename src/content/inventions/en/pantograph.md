# Brief description

A mechanical linkage of parallelograms designed to duplicate, enlarge, or reduce drawings and mechanical motions with precise scale retention.

## Use / Function

- **Primary use**: Scaling and copying technical drawings, maps, and artwork.
- **Secondary uses**: Guiding engraving tools, transferring motion in industrial machinery, pantograph power collection for electric trains.
- **Scale**: Workshop / Drafting desk.

## Operating principle

The pantograph operates on **geometric similarity** and **thales's theorem**:

1.  **Parallelogram Linkage**: Four rigid bars are connected at four pivot points to form a flexible parallelogram structure.
2.  **Collinear Alignment**: The fixed anchor point, the tracing stylus, and the drawing pen must lie along a single straight line.
3.  **Proportional Motion**: When the tracing stylus moves across a template, geometric constraints force the drawing pen to replicate the motion scaled by the ratio of arm lengths.

## How to create it

### Minimum functional version

1.  **Bars**: Cut four straight, equal-width strips of **[Wood](/en/materials/wood)** or **[Metal](/en/materials/metal)**.
2.  **Pivots**: Drill precise holes along the bars at calibrated intervals and join them with smooth-pivoting rivets or screws.
3.  **Anchor**: Secure one end firmly to a heavy base or drawing board.
4.  **Stylus & Lead**: Attach a pointer stylus to the guide point and a **[Graphite](/en/materials/graphite)** pencil or pen to the output point.

### Technical level

Basic to Intermediate. Requires accurate hole placement and low-friction joints.

## Materials needed

- **Essential materials**:
  - **Strips**: Rigid **[Wood](/en/materials/wood)** slats or flat **[Metal](/en/materials/metal)** strips.
  - **Fasteners**: Bolts with locknuts or rivets for smooth articulation.
  - **Marking Element**: **[Graphite](/en/materials/graphite)** stick or pen.
- **Tools**:
  - Ruler, drill, saw, screwdriver.

## Variants and improvements

- **Adjustable Scale Arms**: Multi-hole bars allow quick adjustment of magnification ratios (e.g., 2:1, 4:1).
- **3D Sculpting Pantograph**: Adds spatial gimbals to duplicate three-dimensional relief sculptures into wood or stone.
- **Engraving Pantograph**: Heavy-duty metal pantograph linked to a motorized spindle router for metal sign-making.

## Limits and risks

- **Joint Play**: Any looseness or backlash in the pivot joints causes distortion and inaccuracies in the output drawing.
- **Flexure**: Weak or flexible arms bend during tracing, degrading precision.
- **2D Limitation**: Standard planar pantographs are limited to two-dimensional surfaces.
