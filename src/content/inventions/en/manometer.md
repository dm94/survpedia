# Brief description

A manometer is an instrument used to measure the pressure of fluids (liquids or gases) by balancing a column of fluid against an applied pressure difference. Featuring a U-shaped transparent tube partially filled with liquid, it provides precise measurement of pressure relative to atmospheric pressure or between two distinct fluid lines.

## Use / Function

- **Differential Pressure Measurement:** Measuring pressure drop across filters, orifices, or flow meters in fluid channels.
- **System Calibration:** Calibrating mechanical pressure gauges, regulators, and pneumatic or hydraulic controls.
- **Scale:** Laboratory bench-top U-tube manometers, industrial inclined differential manometers, and HVAC airflow instruments.

## Operating principle

1. **Hydrostatic Equilibrium:** A manometric liquid (such as water or high-density mercury) settles at equal heights in both legs of an open U-tube under atmospheric pressure.
2. **Pressure Difference Application:** Connecting a pressurized gas or liquid source to one leg forces the manometric liquid down in that leg and up in the opposite leg.
3. **Displacement Measurement:** The height difference ($\Delta h$) between the liquid levels in the two columns is directly proportional to the applied pressure difference ($\Delta P = \rho g \Delta h$).
4. **Direct Visual Scale:** The pressure magnitude is read directly off a calibrated linear scale mounted alongside the liquid column.

## How to create it

1. **Form U-Tube:** Carefully heat a straight tube of high-grade **[Glass](/en/materials/glass)** over a flame and bend it smoothly into a U-shape with uniform internal bore diameter.
2. **Mount Frame:** Secure the glass U-tube vertically onto a rigid wooden or metal support board using rubber-padded metal clamps.
3. **Attach Graduated Scale:** Mount a millimeter-graduated ruler or precision scale directly behind the vertical legs of the U-tube.
4. **Fill Manometric Fluid:** Fill the U-tube halfway with a clean manometric fluid (**[Water](/en/materials/water)** for low pressures, or **[Mercury](/en/materials/mercury)** for higher pressure ranges).
5. **Install Fittings:** Fit **[Brass](/en/materials/brass)** connectors and flexible tubing to the top openings of the U-tube for connecting to system pressure ports.

## Materials needed

- **Transparent Column:** Uniform glass tubing. Sourced from **[Glass](/en/materials/glass)**.
- **Manometric Working Liquid:** Water or dense liquid metal. Sourced from **[Water](/en/materials/water)** and **[Mercury](/en/materials/mercury)**.
- **Connections & Mounting:** Metal fittings, wooden board, and brass clamps. Sourced from **[Brass](/en/materials/brass)** and **[Wood](/en/materials/wood)**.

## Variants and improvements

- **U-Tube Manometer:** Standard open-end or differential U-tube configuration; simple and highly reliable reference standard.
- **Well-Type Manometer:** Uses a large reservoir well on one leg so only a single vertical column height needs to be read.
- **Inclined Manometer:** Inclines one leg at an angle to expand the scale length, greatly improving sensitivity for low-velocity airflow measurements.

## Limits and risks

- **Fluid Blowout:** Overpressure exceeding the U-tube column height can force toxic or corrosive manometric fluid out of the tube.
- **Toxicity Hazard:** Using mercury requires strict ventilation and handling to prevent toxic vapor exposure or spills.
- **Meniscus Reading Error:** Parallax errors and surface tension capillary effects can introduce minor reading inaccuracies if bore diameter is inconsistent.
