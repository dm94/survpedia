# Brief description

A fanning mill (or winnowing machine) is a post-harvest agricultural machine that uses a hand-cranked or belt-driven fan combined with oscillating wire sieves to separate threshed grain from chaff, weed seeds, straw fragments, and dust.

## Use / Function

- **Grain Cleaning:** Cleans harvested wheat, barley, rye, oats, and rice after threshing.
- **Seed Sorting:** Sorts seeds by size and weight, selecting heavy, plump seeds for next season's sowing and eliminating weed seeds.
- **Scale:** Farmstead to agricultural cooperative level.

## Operating principle

1. **Feeding:** Threshed grain and chaff are poured into an upper hopper with an adjustable metering slide.
2. **Air Separation (Winnowing):** As grain falls past a hand-cranked paddle fan, a steady airflow blows lighter chaff, dust, and dry straw husks out the back of the machine.
3. **Screening (Sieving):** The heavier grain falls onto a series of inclined, reciprocating wire sieves (riddles) agitated by an eccentric cam mechanism on the fan shaft.
4. **Grading:** Large debris (twigs, unbroken heads) rolls over the top sieve; good grain passes through fine screens into a clean spout, while small weed seeds drop through the bottom screen.

## How to create it

1. **Construct the Frame and Hopper:** Build a sturdy wooden frame from hard **[Wood](/en/materials/wood)** with an upper funnel-shaped hopper equipped with an adjustable slide valve to regulate grain flow.
2. **Build the Rotary Fan:** Mount a hand-cranked wooden paddle fan inside a cylindrical fan housing, connected via a drive belt or gear to double its rotational speed.
3. **Assemble the Oscillating Shoe:** Suspend a movable wooden box (shoe) below the fan exit using flexible wooden springs or leather straps.
4. **Install Sieves and Cam Linkage:** Fit interchangeable woven **[Wire](/en/materials/wire)** or perforated sheet metal sieves in the shoe, and connect the shoe to an eccentric cam on the fan axle so cranking the fan agitates the sieves back and forth.
5. **Add Separation Chutes:** Position inclined wooden chutes beneath the sieves to direct clean grain, tailing chaff, and weed seeds into separate collection buckets.

## Materials needed

- **Frame & Housing:** Hard **[Wood](/en/materials/wood)** (oak, pine) and fasteners.
- **Fan & Shaft:** **[Iron](/en/materials/iron)** or **[Steel](/en/materials/steel)** axle with wooden paddles.
- **Sieves:** Woven **[Wire](/en/materials/wire)** mesh or perforated **[Sheet Metal](/en/materials/sheet-metal)**.
- **Drive Mechanism:** Cast iron gears or wooden pulleys with leather belts.

## Variants and improvements

- **Manual Hand-Cranked Mill:** Operated by one person turning the crank while another feeds the hopper.
- **Power-Driven Mill:** Driven by a water wheel, treadwheel, or small engine.
- **Combined Thresher-Cleaner:** Integrated directly into mechanical **[Threshing Machine](/en/inventions/threshing-machine)** and modern combine harvesters.

## Limits and risks

- **Fan Speed Sensitivity:** Cranking too fast blows light grain away with the chaff; cranking too slowly leaves chaff in the grain.
- **Screen Clogging:** Wet grain or sticky weed seeds quickly clog the fine wire mesh screens.
- **Dust Hazard:** Generates dense airborne agricultural dust, requiring ventilation and dust masks to prevent respiratory irritation.
