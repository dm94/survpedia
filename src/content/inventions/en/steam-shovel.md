# Brief description

A steam shovel is a heavy excavating machine powered by a steam engine. It uses a mechanical bucket or bucket scoop attached to a boom and dipper stick to dig up earth, rock, and loose material, loading it into carts, wagons, or stockpiles.

## Use / Function

- **Primary Use:** Bulk earthmoving, canal digging, railway construction, and open-pit mining.
- **Secondary Uses:** Dredging riverbanks and quarry excavation.
- **Scale:** Heavy industrial and infrastructure scale.

## Operating principle

1. **Power Generation:** A high-pressure steam boiler feeds steam to independent steam engines (or a central engine with clutches and winches).
2. **Hoisting & Crowding:** Cable winches hoist the dipper bucket vertically, while a crowding mechanism pushes the dipper stick horizontally outward into the earth face.
3. **Digging Cycle:** The bucket teeth bite into the material and scoop upward.
4. **Swinging & Dumping:** The entire upper structure (or boom assembly) rotates to align over a dumping wagon, where a latch pin on the bucket door is tripped via cable to discharge the payload.

## How to create it

1. **Fabricate the Chassis:** Build a rigid structural frame from heavy **[Steel](/en/materials/steel)** or **[Iron](/en/materials/iron)** mounted on railway trucks, continuous tracks, or wide wheels.
2. **Mount Boiler & Steam Engine:** Install a high-capacity vertical or horizontal **[Boiler](/en/inventions/boiler)** paired with a multi-cylinder **[Steam Engine](/en/inventions/steam-engine)** setup.
3. **Assemble Boom and Dipper Stick:** Erect a steel lattice or wooden beam boom, attaching a hinged dipper arm equipped with a hard steel bucket and teeth.
4. **Install Winches & Cables:** Rig steel wire ropes or heavy chains over pulley sheaves connected to steam-driven winches for hoisting, crowding, and swinging.
5. **Add Control Station:** Position mechanical levers and pedal linkages for the operator to manipulate winches, swing mechanisms, and the bucket door trip rope.

## Materials needed

- **Structural Framing:** **[Steel](/en/materials/steel)** and heavy **[Iron](/en/materials/iron)** structural channels.
- **Power Unit:** **[Boiler](/en/inventions/boiler)** and **[Steam Engine](/en/inventions/steam-engine)** components.
- **Cables & Rigging:** Steel wire rope or heavy forged **[Chain](/en/inventions/chain)**.
- **Fuel & Water:** **[Coal](/en/materials/coal)** or **[Wood](/en/materials/wood)**, plus clean **[Water](/en/materials/water)** for steam generation.

## Variants and improvements

- **Rail-mounted Shovel:** Early models operated strictly on temporary railway tracks.
- **Crawler Track Shovel:** Replaced rails with continuous tracks, allowing movement on unpaved terrain.
- **Electric & Diesel Draglines / Excavators:** Modern descendants replaced steam boilers with internal combustion or electric motors and hydraulic cylinders.

## Limits and risks

- **Boiler Risks:** Requires strict pressure monitoring to prevent boiler explosion.
- **Mechanical Cables:** High tension on cables presents snapping hazards; regular inspection is critical.
- **Mobility:** Extremely heavy machinery requiring flat or reinforced surfaces to prevent tipping or sinking into soft soil.
