# Brief description

An induction furnace is an electrical furnace in which heat is applied by induction heating of metal. It melts high-purity metals and alloys cleanly without requiring direct fuel combustion or contact with arc electrodes.

## Use / Function

- **High-Purity Melting:** Melts iron, steel, copper, aluminum, and precious metals without fuel contaminants like sulfur or carbon.
- **Precision Alloying:** Produces exact elemental alloys due to precise temperature control and natural electromagnetic stirring.
- **Foundry Casting:** Supplies liquid metal for precision casting, tool manufacturing, and machine parts.

## Operating principle

1. **High-Frequency AC Supply:** High-frequency alternating current is passed through a water-cooled **[Copper](/en/materials/copper)** induction coil.
2. **Magnetic Field Generation:** The current produces a rapidly alternating electromagnetic field inside the coil.
3. **Eddy Currents & Joule Heating:** The magnetic field induces strong eddy currents (Foucault currents) within the conductive metal charge placed inside a refractory crucible.
4. **Melting & Stirring:** Electrical resistance in the metal rapidly converts eddy currents into heat, melting the charge while magnetic forces stir the molten pool naturally.

## How to create it

1. **Form the Induction Coil:** Wind heavy, hollow **[Copper](/en/materials/copper)** tubing into a tight helix to serve as the primary induction winding.
2. **Build the Refractory Crucible:** Mold a high-temperature non-conductive crucible out of refractory **[Clay](/en/materials/clay)**, alumina, or **[Graphite](/en/materials/graphite)** placed inside the copper coil.
3. **Set Up Alternating Power Circuit:** Connect an AC high-frequency generator or inverter system (utilizing high-power **[Vacuum Tube](/en/inventions/vacuum-tube)** or **[Transistor](/en/inventions/transistor)** oscillators) to supply high current.
4. **Integrate Cooling System:** Pump cooling water through the hollow copper tube coil to prevent the winding from overheating.
5. **Enclose and Shield:** Mount the coil assembly inside a heavy **[Steel](/en/materials/steel)** or non-magnetic frame with electrical insulation.

## Materials needed

- **Coil:** Heavy hollow **[Copper](/en/materials/copper)** **[Wire](/en/materials/wire)** / tubing.
- **Crucible:** Refractory **[Clay](/en/materials/clay)** or **[Graphite](/en/materials/graphite)**.
- **Frame & Support:** **[Steel](/en/materials/steel)** structure and ceramic thermal insulation.
- **Power Electronics:** **[Transformer](/en/inventions/transformer)**, **[Capacitor](/en/inventions/capacitor)** bank, and high-frequency inverter.

## Variants and improvements

- **Coreless Induction Furnace:** Coil surrounds an un-cored crucible; highly versatile for melting various alloys and scrap metals.
- **Channel Induction Furnace:** Uses an iron core and channel loop; highly efficient for holding and continuous pouring of molten iron.
- **Vacuum Induction Melting (VIM):** Operates inside a vacuum chamber to eliminate gas absorption when processing reactive alloys.

## Limits and risks

- **Electrical Energy Requirement:** Demands substantial high-frequency electrical power; unusable without robust generators.
- **Molten Metal Explosion:** Water contamination in charge materials can cause violent steam explosions in molten metal.
- **Electromagnetic Radiation:** Strong high-frequency electromagnetic fields require proper shielding and precautions.
