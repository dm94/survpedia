# Brief description

A facility or mechanical device designed to process harvested tree trunks into dimensional timber, boards, and beams using power-driven saw blades. Driven by water wheels, wind turbines, or steam engines, the sawmill transformed timber processing from laborious manual pit sawing into a high-throughput industrial cornerstone.

## Use / Function

- **Primary Use:** Rapid conversion of raw timber logs into standardized construction lumber, planks, and beams.
- **Secondary Uses:** Processing wood waste (sawdust and offcuts) for charcoal production, paper pulp, or boiler fuel.
- **Scale:** Community watermills to industrial steam-powered lumber complexes.

## Operating principle

1.  **Motive Power:** Mechanical energy from a **[Water Wheel](/en/inventions/water-wheel)**, **[Windmill](/en/inventions/windmill)**, or **[Steam Engine](/en/inventions/steam-engine)** drives a central drive shaft.
2.  **Motion Conversion:** A crank or eccentric mechanism transforms rotary shaft motion into reciprocating up-and-down sawing action, or directly spins a circular blade.
3.  **Log Feeding:** A ratcheted carriage mechanism advances the heavy timber log into the moving blade step-by-step with each stroke.
4.  **Cutting:** High-speed steel or iron saw teeth slice through wood fibers along the grain, producing uniform boards.

## How to create it

1.  **Frame & Carriage:** Construct a sturdy heavy timber bed and carriage with iron rails to hold and advance logs straight into the saw blade.
2.  **Saw Mechanism:** Mount a long, thick pit-saw blade vertically inside a rigid wooden frame (sash saw) connected to a pitman arm, or mount a circular blade on an arbor.
3.  **Drive Linkage:** Connect a crankshaft to the motive source (such as a waterwheel axle) using leather belts or iron gears.
4.  **Feed Mechanism:** Install a pawl-and-ratchet gear driven by the saw frame's movement to automatically advance the carriage forward on each upstroke.

## Materials needed

- **Frame & Carriage:** Heavy **[Wood](/en/materials/wood)** timber and **[Iron](/en/materials/iron)** rails/hardware.
- **Blades:** Hardened **[Steel](/en/materials/steel)** or wrought iron with tempered steel teeth.
- **Linkage & Bearings:** **[Cast Iron](/en/materials/cast-iron)** crankshafts and bronze or wooden bearings.
- **Power Coupling:** Leather drive belts or iron gearing.

## Variants and improvements

- **Pit Sawing:** Hand-operated two-person saw pit (pre-industrial predecessor).
- **Sash Sawmill:** Single vertical blade held in a frame, driven by a simple waterwheel crank.
- **Gang Sawmill:** Multiple parallel blades fixed in one frame to cut an entire log into multiple planks in a single pass.
- **Circular Sawmill:** Spinning circular blade allowing continuous, high-speed cutting without reciprocating dead stops.
- **Band Sawmill:** Continuous flexible steel band running over two wheels for thin kerf cuts and minimal wood waste.

## Limits and risks

- **Kickback and Blade Shatter:** Fast-moving blades can shatter or throw wood under stress; protective shielding is essential.
- **Mechanical Wear:** High friction generates heat, dulling teeth quickly and requiring frequent filing and tension adjustment.
- **Fire Hazard:** Accumulation of dry sawdust near high-friction bearings poses a severe fire risk.
