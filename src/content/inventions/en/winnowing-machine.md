# Brief description

A winnowing machine (or winnowing fan / winnower) is an agricultural machine that separates harvested grain from chaff, straw, and dust using forced airflow generated by a hand-cranked or belt-driven fan combined with reciprocating sieves. It replaced traditional hand winnowing, allowing grain cleaning independent of natural wind conditions.

## Use / Function

- **Grain Cleaning:** Rapidly separates wheat, rice, barley, and oats from lighter chaff, husks, and weed seeds.
- **Post-Harvest Processing:** Prepares clean grain for milling into flour, seed storage, or market distribution.
- **Scale:** Farmstead or village-level agricultural processing.

## Operating principle

1. **Gravity Feed:** Threshed grain mixture is loaded into an upper hopper and fed gradually into the machine.
2. **Forced Air Separation:** A rotating rotary fan creates a steady horizontal airstream across the falling stream of grain.
3. **Aerodynamic Sorting:** Dense heavy grains drop straight down onto collection chutes, while lighter chaff, straw, and empty husks are blown out through the rear exhaust port.
4. **Sieve Grading:** Oscillating wire or perforated sheet metal sieves shake out oversized straw debris and undersized weed seeds.

## How to create it

1. **Construct the Wooden Frame:** Build a sturdy rectangular frame out of **[Wood](/en/materials/wood)** to house the fan drum and sieve chamber.
2. **Build the Rotary Fan:** Mount four or six wooden or canvas paddles made of **[Wood](/en/materials/wood)** and **[Fabric](/en/materials/fabric)** onto a central axle turned by a hand crank or pulley.
3. **Enclose the Fan Housing:** Build a curved wooden shroud around the fan to direct airflow into a focused horizontal stream towards the grain chute.
4. **Install Slanted Sieves:** Suspend a set of interchangeable wire or mesh sieves on flexible wooden spring hangers linked to an eccentric crank arm on the fan shaft for shaking action.
5. **Add Feed Hopper and Outlets:** Mount a top feeding hopper with an adjustable slide gate made of **[Iron](/en/materials/iron)** or **[Wood](/en/materials/wood)** and position sloping grain discharge chutes below.

## Materials needed

- **Structural Frame & Blades:** Seasoned **[Wood](/en/materials/wood)** boards and framing timber.
- **Fan Paddles & Fittings:** **[Fabric](/en/materials/fabric)** or lightweight wooden vanes, **[Iron](/en/materials/iron)** axle, hand crank, bearings, and mesh sieves.

## Variants and improvements

- **Combined Winnower and Screen:** Features multiple stacked vibrating sieves of varying mesh sizes to simultaneously clean and grade grain by kernel size.
- **Power-Driven Winnower:** Connected via belt drive to a stationary steam engine, water wheel, or tractor PTO shaft for high-volume grain handling.
- **Combine Harvester Integration:** Internal winnowing mechanisms form a core component of modern self-propelled combine harvesters.

## Limits and risks

- **Airspeed Calibration:** Excessive fan speed blows light grain out with the chaff; insufficient airflow leaves dirty grain full of husks.
- **Dust Hazard:** Operating the machine releases airborne chaff dust, requiring respiratory protection or outdoor operation.
- **Moisture Sensitivity:** Damp grain or chaff clogs the sieves and reduces separation efficiency.
