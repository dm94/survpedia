# Brief description

A Roots blower (or rotary lobe blower) is a positive displacement rotary pump that moves fluids or gases using a pair of meshing lobed rotors. Operating without internal compression, it delivers a constant volume of air per revolution, making it an essential air booster for metallurgical furnaces, gasifiers, and forced ventilation systems.

## Use / Function

- **Blast Furnace & Forge Blower:** Providing high-volume forced air combustion draft to **[Blast Furnace](/en/inventions/blast-furnace)** and **[Cupola Furnace](/en/inventions/cupola-furnace)** smelting setups.
- **Engine Supercharging:** Supplying forced air charge to internal combustion engines to increase volumetric efficiency and power output.
- **Pneumatic Conveying & Aeration:** Pushing granular bulk materials through pipes and aerating wastewater treatment tanks or fermentation vessels.
- **Gas Circulation:** Transporting synthesis gas in **[Wood Gasifier](/en/inventions/wood-gasifier)** systems and chemical processing lines.

## Operating principle

1. **Dual Rotor Setup:** Two figure-eight or figure-three shaped lobed rotors are mounted on parallel shafts inside an oval housing.
2. **Synchronized Rotation:** External timing gears spin the rotors in opposite directions with precise, non-contact clearance between lobes and casing.
3. **Trapping Volume:** As each lobe passes the inlet port, air is trapped in the pocket between the rotor lobe and outer housing wall.
4. **Positive Displacement:** The rotating lobes carry the trapped air pocket around the perimeter to the outlet port, forcing it out into the discharge pipe under pressure.

## How to create it

1. **Cast & Machine the Housing:** Cast a heavy **[Cast Iron](/en/materials/cast-iron)** oval casing with flat end plates and precision-bored inlet and outlet ports.
2. **Fabricate the Lobed Rotors:** Machine two identical two-lobe or three-lobe rotors from **[Cast Iron](/en/materials/cast-iron)** or **[Steel](/en/materials/steel)** with accurate epicycloid or involute profiles.
3. **Mount Bearings & Shafts:** Fit high-precision shafts into side end plates lined with **[Bronze](/en/materials/bronze)** sleeve bearings or roller bearings.
4. **Install Timing Gears:** Mount a pair of identical helical spur gears made of **[Steel](/en/materials/steel)** or **[Brass](/en/materials/brass)** on the external rotor shaft ends to lock the rotors at a strict $90^\circ$ phase shift.
5. **Drive Connection:** Connect a drive pulley or coupling to one shaft to power the blower via steam engine, motor, or hand crank.

## Materials needed

- **Housing & Rotors:** Rigid **[Cast Iron](/en/materials/cast-iron)** or machined structural **[Steel](/en/materials/steel)**.
- **Timing Gears:** **[Steel](/en/materials/steel)** or **[Brass](/en/materials/brass)** precision gears.
- **Bearings & Seals:** **[Bronze](/en/materials/bronze)** bushings, oil seals, and gasket sheets.
- **Drive System:** V-belt pulley or direct shaft coupling.

## Variants and improvements

- **Two-Lobe vs. Three-Lobe:** Three-lobe rotors with twisted helical lobes reduce pressure pulsation, air turbulence, and mechanical noise compared to straight two-lobe designs.
- **Liquid Ring Blower:** Incorporates a liquid sealant film to achieve higher pressure differentials and handle wet gas mixtures.
- **Multistage Blowers:** Stacking two blowers in series allows higher pressure ratios for industrial vacuum systems or deep aeration tanks.

## Limits and risks

- **No Internal Compression:** Because air is compressed only upon entering the discharge pipe against backpressure, thermal efficiency drops at high pressure ratios ($>1\text{ bar}$).
- **Overheating Under Load:** Continuous operation against high resistance generates significant heat, which can cause rotor expansion and binding.
- **Precision Clearance Required:** Excessive rotor clearance allows air backflow (slip), drastically lowering output efficiency; insufficient clearance causes catastrophic rotor contact.
