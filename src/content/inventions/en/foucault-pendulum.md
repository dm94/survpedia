# Brief description

A Foucault pendulum is a simple scientific device—consisting of a heavy, symmetrical sphere suspended by a very long wire from a frictionless swivel—that directly demonstrates the Earth's rotation on its axis without relying on astronomical observations.

## Use / Function

- **Experimental Physics**: Providing unambiguous physical proof of the Earth's diurnal rotation.
- **Geodesy and Latitude Measurement**: Determining local geographic latitude by measuring the rate of precessional rotation of the pendulum's swing plane.
- **Educational Demonstration**: Visualizing inertia, Coriolis force, and rotating frames of reference.
- **Scale**: Large-scale architectural installations (typically requiring ceilings 10 to 60 meters high).

## Operating principle

The mechanism operates based on Newton's First Law of Motion (inertia) within a rotating frame of reference (the Earth).

1. **Inertial Swing Plane**: Once set in motion, the plane of oscillation of a freely swinging pendulum remains fixed in absolute space due to inertia.
2. **Earth's Rotation**: As the Earth rotates beneath the suspended pendulum, observers on the ground turn with the Earth while the pendulum maintains its fixed plane.
3. **Apparent Precession**: To an observer on Earth, the swing plane appears to rotate continuously clockwise in the Northern Hemisphere and counterclockwise in the Southern Hemisphere.
4. **Latitude Dependency**: The precession rate equals $360^\circ \times \sin(\phi)$ per sidereal day, where $\phi$ is the geographical latitude. At the poles ($\phi = 90^\circ$), a full rotation takes 24 hours; at the equator ($\phi = 0^\circ$), no precession occurs.

## How to create it

1. **Fabricating the Bob**: Cast or turn a heavy, perfectly symmetrical metallic sphere (15 to 50 kg) of dense brass or lead. Ensure a central mounting point at the top and a sharp needle pointer at the bottom.
2. **Preparing the Pivot**: Install a rigid support beam at a high elevation (e.g., inside a tower or tall atrium). Mount a knife-edge pivot or high-precision gimbal bearing to minimize directional friction.
3. **Suspending the Wire**: Attach a long, high-tensile steel wire (typically 15 to 30 meters long) from the pivot to the top of the bob. Long wire length minimizes angular swing amplitude, reducing ellipticity.
4. **Setting up the Floor Indicator**: Construct a circular ring or compass dial on the floor directly beneath the bob, lined with sand pins or markers to record the changing swing alignment.
5. **Release Mechanism**: Pull the bob to one side, secure it with a cotton thread, allow it to come to absolute rest, and burn the thread with a match to initiate pure linear oscillation without lateral motion.

## Materials needed

- **Essential materials**:
  - **[Lead](/en/materials/lead)** or **[Brass](/en/materials/brass)**: High density to minimize aerodynamic drag relative to mass.
  - **[Wire](/en/materials/wire)** or **[Steel Cable](/en/materials/steel-cable)**: High-tensile stainless steel wire to support the heavy bob without stretching.
- **Tools**:
  - **[Lathe](/en/inventions/lathe)**: For precision machining of the symmetrical spherical bob.
  - **[Plumb Bob](/en/inventions/plumb-bob)** or **[Spirit Level](/en/inventions/spirit-level)**: For aligning the central rest point accurately over the floor dial.
- **Possible substitutes**:
  - Cast iron or bronze sphere: Suitable alternatives for bob material.

## Variants and improvements

- **Electromagnetically Driven Pendulum**: Uses an electromagnetic ring impulse at the center of swing to counteract air resistance and sustain perpetual oscillation without affecting swing direction.
- **Charron Ring**: A small collar surrounding the top wire that cancels elliptical orbits and enforces strictly planar swinging.
- **Short Foucault Pendulum**: Uses electronic sensors and feedback coils to demonstrate precession in compact ceiling heights (2 to 3 meters).

## Limits and risks

- **Elliptical Motion**: Any lateral push or uneven pivot friction introduces elliptical motion, causing spurious precession that masks the Earth's true rotational effect.
- **Air Resistance**: Aerodynamic drag gradually damps the amplitude of swing, requiring continuous electromagnetic impulses for multi-day operation.
- **Building Vibration**: Structural swaying or drafts from ventilation systems perturb the pendulum's motion.
