# Brief description

A nitrary (or saltpeter bed) is a traditional chemical-biological process used to synthesize potassium nitrate (saltpeter) by cultivating nitrifying bacteria in heaps of organic manure, urine, and alkaline earth mortar, followed by aqueous leaching and potash treatment.

## Use / Function

- **Gunpowder Production:** Supplies purified **[Potassium Nitrate](/en/materials/potassium_nitrate)** (75% of gunpowder by weight) without relying on natural cave deposits or foreign imports.
- **Nitric Acid Production:** Serves as the key reagent for distilling **[Nitric Acid](/en/materials/nitric-acid)** for industrial explosives and metallurgy.
- **Agricultural Fertilizer:** Provides concentrated nitrate nitrogen for crop fertilizing.

## Operating principle

1. **Ammonification:** Decomposer microbes break down organic nitrogen from urine and manure into ammonia. **Nitrification:** Soil nitrifying bacteria (*Nitrosomonas* and *Nitrobacter*) oxidize the resulting ammonia through nitrite to nitrate.
2. **Neutralization:** Calcium carbonate from limestone, mortar, or old plaster reacts with nitric acid formed by bacteria to produce soluble calcium nitrate.
3. **Leaching:** Water poured through the aged manure heap washes out the soluble calcium and sodium nitrate salts into collection vats.
4. **Metathesis (Double Displacement):** Wood ash rich in potash (**[Potash](/en/materials/potash)**) is mixed into the liquid extract. Potassium replaces calcium, precipitating insoluble calcium carbonate and leaving potassium nitrate in solution.
5. **Crystallization:** The solution is boiled down and cooled; potassium nitrate crystallizes out as needle-like white crystals.

## How to implement

### 1. Construct the Nitrary Heap
- Build a sheltered, roofed earthen bed protected from rain but open to air circulation.
- Layer animal **[Manure](/en/materials/manure)**, urine, slaughterhouse waste, straw, and crushed **[Lime](/en/materials/lime)** or old mortar.

### 2. Maturation & Aeration
- Keep the heap moist by regularly pouring stagnant urine or dung water over it.
- Turn and loosen the heap every few weeks with shovels to maintain oxygenation for aerobic bacteria. Allow to age for 12 to 24 months.

### 3. Leaching & Conversion
- Pack the mature nitrated earth into wooden tubs with perforated bottoms lined with straw.
- Pour boiling **[Water](/en/materials/water)** over the earth to dissolve the nitrate salts.
- Add concentrated **[Potash](/en/materials/potash)** solution made from leached wood **[Ash](/en/materials/ash)** to convert calcium nitrate into potassium nitrate.

### 4. Purification
- Filter out the chalk precipitate (**[Limestone](/en/materials/limestone)**).
- Boil down the remaining clear liquid until a skin forms on top, then cool slowly to collect pure potassium nitrate crystals.

## Materials needed

- **Organic Nitrogen:** Animal **[Manure](/en/materials/manure)**, urine, and organic refuse.
- **Alkaline Base:** Crushed **[Limestone](/en/materials/limestone)**, slaked **[Lime](/en/materials/lime)**, or mortar scrapings.
- **Potash Source:** Wood **[Ash](/en/materials/ash)** (rich in potassium carbonate).
- **Solvent & Containers:** **[Water](/en/materials/water)**, wooden leaching tubs, and iron boiling kettles.

## Variants and improvements

- **Wall Nitrary (Swiss Method):** Building porous lime-mortar brick walls exposed to stables to absorb nitrogenous vapors directly.
- **French Plantation Method:** Highly organized state-managed saltpeter beds used extensively during the Napoleonic Wars to sustain military ammunition.
- **Haber-Bosch & Ostwald Processes:** Modern synthetic chemistry methods that completely superseded agricultural nitraries by synthesizing ammonia and nitric acid directly from atmospheric nitrogen.

## Limits and risks

- **Time-Intensive:** Requires 1 to 2 years for bacteria to build up harvestable concentrations of nitrates.
- **Odor & Sanitation:** Generates unpleasant odors and biohazards requiring placement downwind from living quarters.
- **Purity Requirements:** Impure saltpeter containing magnesium or calcium chlorides absorbs moisture from the air, rendering gunpowder damp and useless.
